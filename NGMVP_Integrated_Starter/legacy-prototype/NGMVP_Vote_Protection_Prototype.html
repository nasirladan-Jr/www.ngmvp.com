Our mission

Protect the process.
Respect every citizen.

NGMVP is designed to support peaceful civic participation and accountable election monitoring. Our approach is non-violent, evidence-led, and focused on the integrity of the electoral process—not on promoting or suppressing any candidate or party.

♧

Citizen incident reporting

Provide a structured way to record suspected intimidation, vote buying, violence, obstruction, or other election-related irregularities.

⌖

Location-based organization

Sort reports by state, local government area, and polling-unit details so authorized reviewers can prioritize follow-up.

▣

Evidence and review

Support responsible evidence handling, reviewer notes, report status tracking, and a clear record of follow-up actions.

♙

Observer coordination

Provide a future workflow for registering, verifying, training, and assigning observers who have the required authorization.

◷

Timely escalation

Help direct credible reports to appropriate election officials, accredited observer organizations, or emergency services where necessary.

◎

Privacy by design

Limit unnecessary personal information, protect sensitive reports, and avoid publicly exposing voters, witnesses, or alleged victims.

How it works

From observation to responsible action.

Good reporting is specific, safe, and verifiable. A submitted report is a lead for review—not a verdict.

Observe safely

Do not confront anyone, interfere with voting, trespass, or put yourself or others at risk to collect evidence.

Record the facts

Note what you personally observed, when and where it happened, and whether any supporting material exists.

Review and refer

Authorized reviewers assess reports, protect sensitive details, and refer credible concerns through appropriate channels.

Incident desk

See something concerning? Document it responsibly.

Use this form to prepare a report for review. Do not submit passwords, voter-card numbers, bank details, or unnecessary private information.

  • Be factual and specific.
  • Distinguish what you witnessed from what someone told you.
  • Do not upload or publish another person's sensitive data without a lawful basis.
  • For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate authorities.
This demonstration does not transmit reports to an organization or emergency service. Do not enter real or sensitive incident details until a secure backend is connected.
